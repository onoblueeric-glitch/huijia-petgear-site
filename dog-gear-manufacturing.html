From brief to shipment preparation

A clear route from sample to order.

The exact operations depend on the construction, materials and customization of each product.

  1. 01

    Confirm the specification

    Review product structure, sizes, materials, colors, hardware, logo placement and packaging.

  2. 02

    Develop & approve the sample

    Check the sample and confirm the details that will guide bulk production.

  3. 03

    Prepare components

    Organize webbing, fabric panels, buckles, hooks, labels and other required parts.

  4. 04

    Sew & assemble

    Stitch, reinforce and assemble components to match the approved construction.

  5. 05

    Inspect the finished products

    Check workmanship, dimensions, hardware, appearance and agreed inspection points.

  6. 06

    Label & pack the order

    Prepare size labels, retail packaging, quantities and export cartons to the order requirements.

Questions about production.

Practical information for purchasing teams reviewing the factory, sampling and order requirements.

Prepare your project brief
Where is the HUIJIA PET factory located?

The factory is located in Shangrao, Jiangxi, China. Alibaba Deep Verification Report HGHWT00372508 records the business as a manufacturer with its own factory.

How large is the HUIJIA PET factory?

The verified operating area totals 3,000 square metres, including 2,200 square metres of factory space, 700 square metres of warehouse space and 100 square metres of office space.

What production equipment does HUIJIA PET operate?

The verification report records 58 production machines, including 30 webbing looms, 8 computerized flat sewing machines, 6 bar-tacking machines, 5 pattern sewing machines, 4 binding machines, 4 synchronous sewing machines and 1 laser machine.

How long does factory sampling take?

The verified standard sampling cycle is 7 days per item after the required product details are confirmed. Complex new structures may need a project-specific schedule.

Can buyers request custom inspection and packing requirements?

Yes. Buyers can define inspection checkpoints, labeling, retail packaging and export-carton requirements around the approved product specification.
