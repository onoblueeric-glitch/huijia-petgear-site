PRODUCTION ROUTE

From Buyer Brief to Packed Dog Gear

Each order follows a specification-led route. The exact sequence can change by product construction, materials and customization, but the commercial checkpoints remain clear for the buyer.

Step 1

Specification

Confirm structure, sizes, materials, colors, hardware, logo and packaging.

Step 2

Sampling

Develop and review the product before bulk-production approval.

Step 3

Preparation

Prepare webbing, fabric panels, hardware, labels and production details.

Step 4

Sewing & Assembly

Stitch, reinforce and assemble components to the approved specification.

Step 5

Inspection

Review workmanship, measurements, components and buyer-defined checkpoints.

Step 6

Packing

Apply labels, retail packaging and export-carton requirements.

BUYER-DEFINED CHECKPOINTS

Inspection & Product Testing Support

Inspection requirements can cover dimensions, stitching, reinforcement, hardware, appearance, labeling and packing. Pull-strength testing can be arranged around the product specification and buyer requirements.

FACTORY FAQ

What B2B Buyers Usually Ask

Specific answers for brands, wholesalers and distributors evaluating a dog gear manufacturing partner.

About HUIJIA PET →
Where is the HUIJIA PET factory located?

The factory is located in Shangrao, Jiangxi, China. Alibaba Deep Verification Report HGHWT00372508 records the business as a manufacturer with its own factory.

How large is the HUIJIA PET factory?

The verified operating area totals 3,000 m², including 2,200 m² of factory space, 700 m² of warehouse space and 100 m² of office space.

What production equipment does HUIJIA PET operate?

The report records 58 machines: 30 webbing looms, 8 computerized flat sewing machines, 6 bar-tacking machines, 5 pattern sewing machines, 4 binding machines, 4 synchronous sewing machines and 1 laser machine.

How long does factory sampling take?

The verified standard sampling cycle is 7 days per item after the required product details are confirmed. Complex new structures may need a project-specific schedule.

Can buyers request custom inspection and packing requirements?

Yes. Buyers can define inspection checkpoints, labeling, retail packaging and export-carton requirements around the approved product specification.
