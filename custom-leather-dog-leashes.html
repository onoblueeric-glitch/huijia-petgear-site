Custom Leather Dog Leashes

Build a leather leash collection with HUIJIA PET for your pet brand, wholesale range or coordinated collar-and-leash program. Send your design, quantity and target market for a construction review and quotation. Leather type, thickness, edge treatment and hardware are approved together on the sample; a color name alone does not define the finished product.

Discuss Your Order

Leather leash programs for your collection

Everyday loop-handle leash

A fixed-length strap with a hand loop and snap hook. Specify finished length, strap width and thickness, then review the folded handle and hook attachment.

Short traffic or training lead

A shorter working length changes the handle space and distance from the dog. State the measurement endpoints and intended application instead of relying on a retail size label.

Matching leather collar and leash set

Coordinate leather color, grain, edge finish, stitching and hardware across both pieces. Collar size ranges and leash lengths remain separate specifications.

Specify Your Product

Order decisionDetails to approve
Leather specificationIdentify the leather type, surface finish, thickness range, softness and color reference. Approve natural variation and any backing or lining on physical samples.
Edges and stitchingSpecify cut or finished edges, edge color, thread color and the construction at folds. Review both the face and underside for roughness, exposed edges and uneven finishing.
Hook and reinforcementConfirm hook opening, swivel, metal and finish, plus the sewn, riveted or combined attachment. Match hardware weight to the intended product and assess the complete assembly.
Length and widthState whether length includes the hook and handle. Provide units and tolerances. Custom length dog leashes need the same measurement definition at sampling and inspection.
BrandingChoose a logo position and discuss debossing, embossing or a suitable engraved component. Provide artwork size, placement and finish; different names on each item require a separate personalization file.
PackingDefine individual or set packing, care text, barcode, variant label and carton quantity. Confirm material identification before printing retail claims.

Personalized leather leashes and bulk ordering

For custom engraved leather dog leashes, first identify the engraving surface: leather strap, metal plate or hardware. These methods create different results. The approval sample should show legibility, placement, contrast and the back of any attached plate. A logo repeated across an order and a different pet name on every piece are separate production requirements.

Send your order split by color, length and branding option. HUIJIA PET is based in China; destination and freight terms belong in the quote. Our leash programs start from 100 pieces per style, with the selected leather, component minimums, development costs and variant split confirmed in writing. Sampling and production dates depend on the approved specification.

Buyer Questions

Can I order wholesale leather dog leashes with matching collars?

Yes. Submit both specifications together, including collar size ranges, leash length, leather finish, hardware and quantity per variant. Confirm the sample match and the quoted minimum for each item or set.

Do you offer a fixed leather grade or strength rating?

The leather specification and any required performance claim are confirmed for the order. Do not infer a grade, breaking strength or dog-weight rating from appearance; agree the complete-assembly test and acceptance criteria before approval.

Can each leash have a different name?

Submit the name list, character limits, typeface and application surface with your brief. Per-piece personalization, artwork handling and pricing must be confirmed before the order.

What should I check on the sample?

Check finished dimensions, leather feel and variation, edge finish, stitching, handle comfort, rivet or seam placement, hook operation, branding and packaging against the written specification.
