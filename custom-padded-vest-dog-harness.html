PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Build a Comfort-Led Padded Vest Harness Collection

HUIJIA PET develops padded vest dog harnesses for private-label pet brands, wholesalers and retail programs. Vest construction distributes contact across a larger body area and can be adjusted around the required comfort, support, climate and price tier.

Programs can combine soft outer fabric, breathable mesh lining, foam or neoprene padding, adjustable webbing, reinforced leash points, exclusive prints, logo patches and coordinated packaging. Fit and size grading are reviewed during sampling before the specification is released for bulk production.

BUYER FAQ

Custom Padded Vest Dog Harness Manufacturer Questions

Direct answers for pet brands and wholesale buyers planning a custom program.

What materials can be used for a padded vest dog harness?

Options include printed polyester, sandwich mesh, soft lining, neoprene, foam padding, nylon or polyester webbing and plastic or metal hardware.

Can HUIJIA PET develop an exclusive print?

Yes. Buyers can provide artwork or brand guidelines for pattern scaling, color review and application across the harness, collar, leash and packaging.

What is the MOQ for a padded vest harness?

Selected existing styles start from 100 pcs per style. Custom logo or color programs typically start from 300 pcs per style per color, and fully custom designs typically start from 500 pcs per design.

How is vest-harness fit approved?

The sample is reviewed for neck and chest proportions, adjustment range, contact areas, buckle position, leash points, workmanship and size grading before bulk approval.

What is the typical production lead time?

Bulk production normally takes 25–35 days after sample approval. Complex prints, special hardware or custom packaging may require 35–45 days.
