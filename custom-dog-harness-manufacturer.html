FROM BRIEF TO BULK

OEM/ODM Dog Harness Development Process

A clear approval route keeps product specifications, commercial requirements and bulk manufacturing aligned.

Step 1

Product Brief

Share reference products, target use, size range, estimated quantity, market and target price.

Step 2

Structure & Materials

Confirm construction, fabrics, webbing, padding, hardware, colors, logo and packaging.

Step 3

Prototype Sample

Review fit, adjustment, clip position, workmanship, branding and packaging details.

Step 4

Pre-Production Approval

Freeze the approved specification, size chart, color references and quality requirements.

Step 5

Bulk Production

Manufacture against the approved sample with in-process checks, final inspection and packing.

BUYER FAQ

Ready-Stock & Custom Dog Harness Questions

Answers for buyers starting with stock wholesale and planning a future private-label or OEM program.

What types of custom dog harnesses can HUIJIA PET manufacture?

HUIJIA PET develops no-pull, step-in, vest, mesh, neoprene, tactical, outdoor and fashion dog harnesses for private-label and wholesale programs.

Can you manufacture a dog harness from our design or reference sample?

Yes. Buyers can provide drawings, reference products, brand guidelines or a product brief. We review structure, fit, materials, hardware, size grading and target price before sampling.

Can we mix colors and sizes in a ready-stock dog harness order?

Yes. Ready-stock wholesale starts from 30 pcs per style and can mix available colors and sizes, with at least 3 pcs per selected SKU combination. Current inventory is confirmed before order.

What is the MOQ for private-label and custom dog harness orders?

Simple branded packaging starts from 100 pcs per style, logo applications from 200 pcs, exclusive printed artwork from 300 pcs per design, and fully custom structures or hardware from 500 pcs.

Do you provide custom logo and retail packaging?

Yes. Options include woven labels, rubber patches, embroidery, metal plates, printed patterns, hangtags, inserts, polybags and retail boxes.

How does dog harness sampling and production work?

The standard route covers product brief review, material and structure confirmation, prototype sampling, buyer approval, bulk production, inspection and export packing.

How is bulk dog harness quality controlled?

Quality control can include incoming material checks, in-line workmanship inspection, measurement and fit checks, stitching and hardware review, pull-strength testing against buyer requirements, final inspection and packing verification.
