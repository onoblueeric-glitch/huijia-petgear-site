Build a Printed Harness Range Without a New Structure

This printed H-style dog harness gives pet brands and distributors a straightforward base product for seasonal, fashion and value-led collections. Six existing artwork directions make it easier to test multiple looks while keeping the structure and size grading consistent.

The design uses adjustable webbing straps, sliders, a metal buckle and dual metal D-rings. Buyers can begin with shown patterns or develop exclusive artwork, logo patches, coordinated collars and leashes, branded hangtags and retail packaging. Final materials, measurements, hardware finishes and workmanship standards are frozen in the approved sample before bulk production.

BUYER FAQ

Printed H-Style Dog Harness Questions

Direct answers for brands, wholesalers and distributors planning a printed harness program.

What sizes are available?

The standard range includes S, M, L and XL, with adjustable ranges from 30–40 cm through 55–80 cm. Select by measured girth first; weight guidance is approximate.

Can we mix the six shown patterns?

Yes. A mixed pattern and size assortment can be reviewed during quotation, subject to current material availability and order quantity.

Can you add our logo or exclusive pattern?

Yes. Options can include woven labels, rubber patches, printed logos, exclusive webbing artwork, coordinated hardware and retail packaging.

Is this harness escape-proof?

No harness is escape-proof for every dog. This adjustable H-style structure supports an escape-resistant fit when correctly sized and adjusted. Fit, use conditions and testing requirements should be confirmed for your market.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the selected pattern or custom artwork, quantity by size and color, logo method, packaging requirements, target market and delivery date.
