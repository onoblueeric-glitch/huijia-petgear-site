Buyer Questions

Can artwork continue across a leash and matching collar?

Yes. The artwork scale, direction, colors and webbing width are reviewed for each product so the collection can be approved together.

Is the bag holder included with every leash?

The bag holder is a separate development option. The quotation and packing specification must identify whether it is included in a bundle.

What should I provide for a printed leash quotation?

Send the artwork, finished length, webbing width, hardware requirements, quantity by design and color, logo method, packaging and target market.

What are the MOQ and delivery time?

MOQ, sample timing and production timing are quoted for the selected material, dimensions, colors, hardware and packaging. Send the quantity by variant and requested delivery date for confirmation.
