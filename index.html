Procurement FAQ

A few things to know.

Quick answers for pet brands, wholesalers and distributors comparing dog harness, leash and collar manufacturers.

Ask about your project →
Is HUIJIA PET a manufacturer or a trading company?

HUIJIA PET operates its own production factory in Shangrao, Jiangxi, China. The 2026 Alibaba deep-verification report records 3,000 square metres of total operating space, 30 employees and 58 production machines.

What is the MOQ for custom dog harnesses, leashes and collars?

Many standard custom and private-label programs start from 100 pieces per style. The final MOQ depends on materials, hardware, printing, packaging and whether the product uses a ready-stock or fully custom structure.

How long does custom sampling take?

Standard sample development can be completed in about 7 days after materials, dimensions, logo and construction details are confirmed. Complex new structures may require a project-specific schedule.

What private-label options can HUIJIA PET provide?

Buyers can customize colors, webbing, printed patterns, hardware, woven or rubber logos, labels, retail packaging and coordinated harness, leash and collar collections.
