Coated leash constructions to specify

Fixed-length walking leash

A coated strap, loop handle and snap hook create a simple collection format. Approve strap feel, fold construction and edge finish with the selected hardware.

Long-line or custom-length leash

Specify the usable length, total weight target and handle arrangement. Longer straps change packing and handling, so review the complete sample rather than a short material swatch.

Coordinated collar and leash set

Match the coating color and texture while specifying collar adjustment, holes, fittings and leash connections separately. Approve the finished set together.

PVC-coated webbing and BioThane requests

BioThane® is a material brand, not a generic name for every coated leash. A request for a custom BioThane dog leash needs the specified genuine material grade and traceable sourcing to be confirmed before the quote or product labeling uses that name. This page does not claim authorized distributor status or that all coated options use BioThane.

For a PVC dog-coated leash manufacturing brief, specify PVC coating explicitly. If an alternative coating is acceptable, identify which properties and appearance must remain the same and approve a new sample. Do not substitute a generic coated strap for a named-brand material without the buyer’s written agreement.

Order quantities, material minimums, colors, development costs and lead time are confirmed for the selected construction. Include the destination, requested arrival date and packing requirements. See our leash collection for textile alternatives and walking sets for coordinated ranges.

Buyer Questions

Is every coated dog leash made from BioThane?

No. BioThane is a material brand. Generic coated webbing must be identified by its actual construction and coating; genuine branded-material sourcing must be verified for the particular order.

Can I request a custom color or length?

Include the color reference, finished length, width, thickness and quantity split in the brief. Material availability, minimums, tolerances and the final assembly are confirmed during quotation and sample approval.

Does a coated strap make the complete leash waterproof?

The finished leash also includes hardware, connections and any labels. Agree the intended exposure, care instructions and test scope before making a whole-product performance claim.

What should I send for a matching coated collar and leash set?

Send collar sizes and adjustment details, leash dimensions, color and texture, hardware, branding, pack contents and quantities. Confirm the material specification and both assembled samples together.
