Brief Product Description

This padded nylon dog collar combines a straightforward solid-color presentation with everyday adjustment and a soft inner lining. The supplied images show black, gray, red and blue options, each built with woven webbing, a plastic side-release buckle, an adjustment slider and a metal D-ring.

The supplied size guide covers M, L and XL, pairing wider webbing with larger adjustment ranges. The product can support wholesale assortment building or an OEM/private-label program once colors, materials, component grade, logo method, tolerances and packaging are confirmed in an approved specification.

Key Features of the Padded Nylon Collar

· Soft Padded Lining: A dark inner layer adds a comfort-focused finish beneath the outer webbing.

· Adjustable M–XL Sizing: Three size grades cover 30–65 cm across 15, 20 and 25 mm webbing widths.

· Quick-Release Closure: The shown plastic side-release buckle supports convenient everyday fastening.

· Metal Leash D-Ring: A separate metal ring provides the leash connection point.

· Four Solid Colors: Black, gray, red and blue support simple core-range merchandising.

· Private-Label Ready: Review logo labels or patches, custom color, hangtags and retail packaging around the order volume.
