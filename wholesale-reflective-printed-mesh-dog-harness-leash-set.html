Brief Product Description

Developed for pet brands, wholesalers, importers and online retailers, this ready-stock dog walking set pairs a breathable printed mesh vest harness with a coordinated 1.2 m leash. Reflective edge piping improves visibility in low-light walking conditions, while adjustable neck and chest straps support flexible fitting across XS, S, M and L.

The padded body distributes pressure across a broader chest area than a narrow webbing-only harness. A back D-ring provides a practical leash connection, and quick-release buckles make the harness easy to put on. Buyers can test the three shown stock prints or move into exclusive artwork, branded logo patches and retail-ready packaging.

Key Features of the Reflective Mesh Harness Set

· Reflective Edge Trim: Visibility-enhancing piping outlines the harness body for evening and low-light walking collections.

· Breathable Padded Mesh: Soft mesh panels support airflow and everyday comfort for small and medium dogs.

· Four-Point Adjustability: Adjustable neck and chest straps help buyers cover a practical XS–L size range with a closer fit.

· Matching 1.2 m Leash: Each set includes a coordinated printed leash for a unified, retail-ready presentation.

· Three Ready-Stock Prints: Yellow cat, mint bow and purple geometric directions support faster assortment testing.

· Private-Label Ready: Develop a logo patch, exclusive repeat print, custom trim, hardware finish, hangtag and packaging.

Reflective Printed Mesh Dog Harness Set FAQs

Q1: What sizes are available?

The stock range includes XS, S, M and L. Neck adjustment runs from 26–36 cm for XS up to 40–60 cm for L; chest adjustment runs from 25–50 cm up to 55–83 cm. Select by measured neck and chest circumference rather than weight alone.

Q2: Is a leash included?

Yes. Each wholesale set includes one coordinated 1.2 m leash. Exact leash width, snap hook, print and packaging are confirmed with the order.

Q3: Is the trim reflective?

The edge piping is designed to reflect incident light and improve low-light visibility. It is a visibility aid, not a substitute for supervision or dedicated safety lighting. Buyers can request material and performance confirmation for their market.

Q4: Can we mix prints and sizes?

Yes. Ready-stock wholesale starts from 30 sets per style, with available prints and XS–L sizes mixed at a minimum of 3 sets per selected print and size combination, subject to inventory.

Q5: Can we add our logo or artwork?

Yes. Options include woven labels, rubber patches, printed logos, exclusive repeat artwork, coordinated trim colors, branded hangtags and retail packaging, subject to MOQ and artwork review.

Q6: What information is needed for a quote?

Send the selected print, quantity by size, logo method, packaging requirements, target market and required delivery date.

Q8: What inspection support can wholesale buyers request?
