Our company & manufacturing base

Our own factory.
Our people. Our products.

HUIJIA PET is the dog walking gear business of Guangxin District Huijia Pet Products Factory. Based in Shangrao, Jiangxi, our factory develops and manufactures harnesses, collars, leashes and coordinated walking sets.

Our production team works with webbing, fabrics, hardware and branding details, taking products through sample preparation, sewing, assembly, inspection and packing. We support OEM, ODM and private-label orders for pet brands and wholesale buyers.

Is HUIJIA PET a manufacturer or a trading company?

HUIJIA PET operates through Guangxin District Huijia Pet Products Factory in Shangrao, Jiangxi, China. The 2026 Alibaba Deep Verification report identifies the business as a manufacturer with its own factory.

What products does HUIJIA PET manufacture?

HUIJIA PET develops and manufactures custom dog harnesses, leashes, collars and coordinated dog walking collections for pet brands, wholesalers and distributors.

Does HUIJIA PET support OEM and private label orders?

Yes. Buyers can specify product structure, materials, colors, patterns, hardware, branding, labels, retail packaging and coordinated product collections.

How can buyers verify HUIJIA PET's factory information?
