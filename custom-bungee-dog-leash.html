Buyer Questions

Does a bungee leash prevent injuries or stop pulling?

The elastic section can provide extension under tension. It does not guarantee injury prevention or replace training; evaluate the sample for the intended walking use.

Is a load rating published for this leash?

No universal load rating is stated for this reference. Agree the test method, load, extension and acceptance criteria before sampling or production.

Can the elastic response be customized?

The desired extension and force response can be reviewed during development. Feasibility and acceptance depend on the selected materials, construction and sample testing.

What are the MOQ and delivery time?

MOQ, sample timing and production timing are quoted for the selected material, dimensions, colors, hardware and packaging. Send the quantity by variant and requested delivery date for confirmation.
