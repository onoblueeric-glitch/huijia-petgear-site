Printed Bow Tie Dog Collar FAQs

Q1: What sizes are available for this printed bow tie dog collar?

The supplied specification lists S and M. S is 10 mm wide with a 23–30 cm reference range, and M is 15 mm wide with a 29–40 cm reference range. Final measurement method and tolerances are confirmed before production.

Q2: Which patterns are shown?

Twelve supplied options are shown: orange, white, red and black stripe; charcoal, light blue, red and light green plaid; and navy, light purple, yellow and gray chevron. Custom pattern development is subject to material availability, MOQ and sample approval.

Q3: What hardware and construction are shown?

The shown construction combines patterned woven fabric and webbing with a decorative bow, plastic quick-release buckle, plastic adjustment components and a metal leash D-ring. Final fiber, component grade and finish are confirmed with the approved sample.

Q4: Can the pattern and branding be customized?

Yes. Subject to MOQ and development review, buyers can request pattern directions, logo applications, woven labels, hangtags and retail packaging.

Q5: What is the typical wholesale MOQ?

Eligible existing-style wholesale programs typically start from 100 pieces. Custom pattern or branding programs typically start from 300 pieces per style per color. Final MOQ depends on the selected sizes, patterns, branding and packaging.

Q6: What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the required pattern, quantity by size, logo method, packaging requirements, target market and requested delivery date.
