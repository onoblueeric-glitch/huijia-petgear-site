Ombre Gradient Dog Collar FAQs

Q1: What sizes are available for this ombre gradient dog collar?

The supplied specification lists S, M, L and XL. S is 10 mm wide with a 20–30 cm reference range, M is 15 mm wide with a 30–40 cm range, L is 20 mm wide with a 33–50 cm range, and XL is 25 mm wide with a 40–65 cm range. Final measurement method and tolerances are confirmed before production.

Q2: Which gradient colors are shown?

Four supplied color directions are shown: pink/blue, blue/purple/orange, ice blue and purple/blue. Custom color development is subject to material availability, MOQ and sample approval.

Q3: What hardware does the collar use?

The shown construction uses a plastic quick-release buckle, plastic adjustment components and a metal leash D-ring. Final component grade and finish are confirmed with the approved sample.

Q4: Can the colors and branding be customized?

Yes. Subject to MOQ and development review, buyers can request color directions, logo applications, woven labels, hangtags and retail packaging.

Q5: What is the typical wholesale MOQ?

Eligible existing-style wholesale programs typically start from 100 pieces. Custom color or branding programs typically start from 300 pieces per style per color. Final MOQ depends on the selected sizes, colors, branding and packaging.

Q6: What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the required colorway, quantity by size, logo method, packaging requirements, target market and requested delivery date.
