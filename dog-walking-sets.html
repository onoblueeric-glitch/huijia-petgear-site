Give Buyers a Complete Brand Story

A matching dog walking set increases the range a retailer or distributor can buy from one collection. Instead of developing three unrelated products, the harness, leash and collar can share the same visual language and be engineered together around the intended market.

HUIJIA PET helps organize the collection at specification level: material, color standards, pattern scale, hardware finish, logo placement, size range, packaging and carton requirements. This makes the collection easier to sample, review and repeat during bulk production.
