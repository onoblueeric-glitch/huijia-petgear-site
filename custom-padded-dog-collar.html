BUYER FAQ

Custom Padded Dog Collar Manufacturer Questions

Direct answers for pet brands and wholesale buyers planning a custom program.

What padding options are available for custom dog collars?

Padding and lining options are reviewed around comfort, use environment, cleanability, target price and the selected webbing construction.

Can padded dog collars use both plastic and metal buckles?

Yes. Closure type, D-ring, slider and hardware finish can be selected according to the collection positioning and target price.

Can you match a padded collar with a leash?

Yes. Webbing, colors, hardware, logo details and packaging can be coordinated across collars, leashes and harnesses.
