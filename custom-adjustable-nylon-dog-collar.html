BUYER FAQ

Custom Adjustable Nylon Dog Collar Manufacturer Questions

Direct answers for pet brands and wholesale buyers planning a custom program.

What can be customized on an adjustable nylon dog collar?

Buyers can customize webbing, width, color, buckle, D-ring, logo method, size range, labels and retail packaging.

Is this style suitable for wholesale programs?

Yes. The clean adjustable construction can be developed as a scalable core program with coordinated colors, sizes and matching accessories.

Can you add our logo to a nylon dog collar?

Yes. Options can include woven labels, rubber patches, printed webbing, embroidery, metal plates and custom packaging.
