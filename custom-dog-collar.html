COLLAR PROGRAMS

Build a Consistent, Retail-Ready Range

Dog collars may look simple, but webbing width, adjustment range, buckle choice, D-ring position and stitching all affect the final specification. We help buyers define these details before sampling so the approved collar can be reproduced consistently during bulk production.

For brands building a collection, prints, solid colors, hardware finishes and labels can be carried across collars, leashes and harnesses. Packaging can then present the products as one coordinated family rather than unrelated items.

BUYER FAQ

Custom Dog Collar Program Questions

Clear specifications help us confirm the correct sampling scope, MOQ and production schedule for your collar range.

What custom dog collar styles can HUIJIA PET manufacture?

We develop tactical, padded, martingale, printed, wide, lightweight and everyday collars for private-label and wholesale programs.

Can materials, hardware and branding be customized?

Yes. Buyers can specify webbing, padding, width, buckle type, leash ring, colors, patterns, logo method, labels and retail packaging.

What should I provide for a custom dog collar quotation?

Send the collar style, target size range, estimated quantity, target market, logo or artwork files, preferred materials and hardware, packaging request and delivery window.
