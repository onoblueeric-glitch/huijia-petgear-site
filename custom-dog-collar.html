BUYER FAQ

Dog Collar Questions

Useful details for choosing styles, preparing specifications and planning your order.

What custom dog collar styles can HUIJIA PET manufacture?

We develop tactical, padded, martingale, printed, wide, lightweight and everyday collars for private-label and wholesale programs.

Can materials, hardware and branding be customized?

Yes. Buyers can specify webbing, padding, width, buckle type, leash ring, colors, patterns, logo method, labels and retail packaging.

What should I provide for a custom dog collar quotation?

Send the collar style, target size range, estimated quantity, target market, logo or artwork files, preferred materials and hardware, packaging request and delivery window.

What is the MOQ for the listed dog collars?

The listed wholesale collar styles show a starting MOQ of 100 pieces. Custom materials, hardware, branding and packaging may change the order requirement; confirm quantities by size and color.

Which measurements are needed for a collar order?

Provide the minimum and maximum neck adjustment, webbing width and measurement tolerances for each size. Review buckle fit, lining and adjustment on the approved sample.

How do wholesale collars and a custom collar program differ?

Start with a listed style for a wholesale inquiry and confirm its option codes, size quantities and availability. A custom program adds changes to the materials, dimensions, hardware, artwork or packaging, which must be reviewed before quoting. Use our custom dog collar selection guide to compare the decisions.

Does custom branding include a different pet name on every collar?

The programs on this page focus on repeatable brand artwork, logos and bulk production. Individual pet names or phone numbers require a separate review of the application method, character limits and order file. Confirm that service before placing a personalized-name order.
