B2B LEASH DEVELOPMENT

Specify More Than Color and Logo

Leash performance depends on the relationship between material, width, length, stitching, handle comfort and hardware. We help buyers translate a target use and price point into a clear product specification that can be sampled, inspected and repeated in bulk.

Choose a standalone private-label leash or coordinate the webbing, print, hardware finish and packaging with a custom harness and collar. This creates a stronger retail collection and reduces visual inconsistency between suppliers.
