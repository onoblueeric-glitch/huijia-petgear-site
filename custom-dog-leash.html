BUYER FAQ

Dog Leash Questions

Useful details for choosing styles, preparing specifications and planning your order.

What determines the MOQ for a custom dog leash?

Material, dimensions, colors or artwork, hardware, branding, packaging and quantity per variant determine the quoted MOQ. Confirm the selected leash specification before placing an order.

What measurements should I send?

For rope leashes, provide finished length and rope diameter. For flat webbing, provide finished length, width and thickness. For bungee designs, also state unloaded length and the required extension conditions.

Are all shown leashes in stock?

The catalog includes existing wholesale styles and development references. Confirm current stock, the selected SKU and the dispatch date in your quotation.

Can I order matching collars or harnesses?

Yes. Coordinate artwork, colors, hardware and packaging through a shared product brief. Each item and the final selling unit must be identified in the quotation.

What is the starting MOQ for the listed wholesale leashes?

Selected listed styles start from 100 pieces. Custom rope, bungee or multi-dog development programs are quoted to the chosen specification; confirm quantities by color and size.
