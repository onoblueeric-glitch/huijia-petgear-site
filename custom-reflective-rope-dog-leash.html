Buyer Questions

Is the pictured leash supplied in one fixed length?

The finished length and rope diameter are specified for each order. Send your target dimensions and tolerance so the sample can be checked against them.

Does reflective detail mean this leash is safety certified?

Reflective detail describes the product construction. Any visibility, strength or destination-market test needs an agreed method and a product-specific report.

Can the leash use a private-label logo?

Logo tags, labels and packaging can be reviewed with the selected rope, grip and hardware. The method and order minimum are confirmed in the quotation.

What are the MOQ and delivery time?

MOQ, sample timing and production timing are quoted for the selected material, dimensions, colors, hardware and packaging. Send the quantity by variant and requested delivery date for confirmation.
