Cartoon Printed Adjustable Dog Collar FAQs

What options are available for this cartoon printed adjustable dog collar?

This range includes 20 photographed options. Use the option selector to choose a color or design, then specify the size and order quantity. Current availability is confirmed in the quotation.

How should I select the size?

The reference table lists S, M, L, XL. Dimensions are reference measurements. Confirm the measuring points, fit and tolerances with your approved sample.

Can you add our logo and retail packaging?

Yes. Logo application, labels, artwork, hardware and branded packaging are reviewed against the selected construction, quantity and approved sample.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Eligible existing-style programs start from 100 pcs. Custom artwork, hardware and branding may require a higher MOQ; final quantities are confirmed by quotation.

How do I request a sample and bulk quotation?

Send the selected product and option code, sizes, quantity, logo or packaging requirements, target market and delivery date. Samples normally take 7–15 working days and bulk production typically takes 25–35 days after sample approval, subject to materials and order quantity.
