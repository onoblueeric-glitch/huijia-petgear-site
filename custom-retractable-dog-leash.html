Custom Retractable Dog Leash

Develop a private-label retractable leash program with HUIJIA PET. The order brief brings the housing, grip, tape, spring, brake and hook together as one product. Start with the intended use and design requirements, then approve the selected construction, operating instructions and sample before bulk production.

Discuss Your Order

Choose your retractable leash order scope

Branding on an existing construction

Review the available body shape, logo area, shell color and packaging. Confirm which changes can use the selected construction and which need additional development.

Tape and handling specification

Define working extension, tape width, handle opening and grip feel. Inspect how the tape feeds through the outlet and retracts during normal operation.

Custom housing development

A new external shape can affect internal space, tooling and assembly. Submit a drawing or design reference so tooling scope, ownership, costs and sample stages can be quoted.

Specify Your Product

Order decisionDetails to approve
Housing and gripConfirm housing material and surface finish, grip material, handle opening and seam fit. ABS and soft-grip constructions are material options to discuss, not a default specification for every order.
Tape and extensionState tape material, width, color, print and usable extension. Define how extension is measured; the advertised length must match the approved configuration.
Brake and lockReview stopping, holding, release and return operation. Agree function checks on the assembled product rather than evaluating the button appearance alone.
Retraction mechanismSpecify the required operating-cycle test conditions, inspection method and acceptance criteria. A supplier component claim is not a verified result for the complete leash.
Hook connectionConfirm hook opening, swivel operation, finish and tape-end attachment. Include the connection in the sample and any agreed assembly testing.
Labels and packagingConfirm intended user instructions, market language, product identification and verified use limitations. Do not print a dog-weight rating or certification without supporting product-specific evidence.

From specification review to a private-label order

Send target quantity by color, required extension, housing reference, logo file, packaging and destination. We review the configuration and quote the applicable minimum, development or tooling charges, sample stages and production schedule. The standard leash starting MOQ does not automatically apply to a newly tooled retractable mechanism.

Approve functional samples before approving retail artwork. Review tape routing, brake operation, return movement, hook attachment and housing assembly against the agreed checks. If the collection includes a bag dispenser or other attachment, specify its position and verify that it does not obstruct the grip, button or tape outlet.

A retractable leash and an adjustable fixed-length leash perform different jobs. If the buyer needs defined short and long settings without a spring housing, compare the adjustable printed webbing leash. Browse other wholesale leash constructions before finalizing the range.

Buyer Questions

Can the housing carry our brand logo?

Logo location, application method, shell color and packaging can be included in the brief. Approve artwork on the selected housing and confirm any tooling or component requirements in the quote.

Are all retractable leash lengths and load ratings available?

No universal length or load rating is stated for this program. The selected construction, dimensions and supported claims must be confirmed with the sample and product-specific evidence.

Can I buy a retail brand model through this page?

This page is for HUIJIA PET OEM and private-label inquiries. It does not list another brand’s inventory, act as its retailer or confirm compatibility with its parts.

What should a retractable dog leash manufacturer quote include?

Request the exact configuration, quantity split, branding, packing, tooling where needed, sample cost, approved test scope, delivery terms and production schedule. Compare quotes on the same complete-product specification.
