BUYER FAQ

Custom Step-In Dog Harness Manufacturer Questions

Direct answers for pet brands and wholesale buyers planning a custom program.

How does a step-in dog harness differ from an overhead harness?

A step-in harness is designed so the dog places its legs through the openings before the harness closes around the body, which can suit buyers seeking an easy-on product structure.

Can step-in harnesses use custom printed fabric?

Yes. Solid colors, exclusive patterns, printed webbing and matching accessories can be developed around buyer artwork and color standards.

What is the MOQ for a custom step-in harness?

Selected existing styles start from 100 pcs per style. Custom color or branding programs typically start from 300 pcs per style per color, and fully custom designs typically start from 500 pcs per design.

How long does a new sample take?

Existing-style samples normally take 7–10 working days. A new custom step-in structure normally takes 10–15 working days after specifications are confirmed.

Can you develop matching leashes and collars?

Yes. Webbing, prints, hardware finish, logo placement and packaging can be coordinated across a complete dog walking set.
