BUYER FAQ

Custom Martingale Dog Collar Manufacturer Questions

Direct answers for pet brands and wholesale buyers planning a custom program.

What is a custom martingale dog collar program?

It is a collar program developed around a martingale-style webbing and control-chain structure, with buyer-specified size, materials, branding and packaging.

Can the webbing pattern be customized?

Yes. Buyers can provide artwork, reference products, brand guidelines or Pantone colors for the required webbing direction.

What information is needed for a martingale collar quotation?

Send your reference style, target size range, estimated quantity, logo or artwork, hardware preference, packaging request and delivery window.
