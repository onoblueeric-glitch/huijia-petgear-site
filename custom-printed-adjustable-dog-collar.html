Brief Product Description

Model C-T01 is an adjustable printed dog collar developed for pet brands, wholesalers, importers and retail programs. The source specification identifies six artwork directions, four size grades and both metal and plastic quick-release buckle versions.

The collar uses nylon-look printed webbing, an adjustment slider and a metal leash D-ring. Exact fiber composition, hardware finish, finished weight, tolerances and export pack-out are confirmed in the approved specification before bulk production.

Key Features of C-T01 Printed Dog Collar

· Six Verified Print Directions: Triangle, bear, cloud, gradient, camouflage and square artwork support coordinated fashion collections.

· Metal or Plastic Buckle: Select the buckle route around target appearance, weight and price positioning.

· Four Adjustable Sizes: S–XL covers listed neck ranges from 20 cm to 65 cm with width-specific webbing.

· Private-Label Ready: Review exclusive artwork, woven labels, rubber patches, logo applications, hangtags and retail packaging.

· Matching Collection Development: Approved colors and artwork can be reviewed for compatible harness, leash and walking-set programs.

Custom Printed Dog Collar FAQs

Q1: What sizes are available for model C-T01?

The source specification lists S, M, L and XL. Adjustable neck ranges are 20–30 cm, 30–40 cm, 33–50 cm and 40–65 cm respectively, with webbing widths from 1 cm to 2.5 cm.

Q2: Can buyers choose a metal or plastic buckle?

Yes. The source listing identifies C-T01 versions with either a metal buckle or a plastic quick-release buckle. Final hardware finish and configuration are confirmed in the quotation and approved sample.

Q3: Which printed patterns are available?

The listed print directions are gradient, camouflage, cloud, square, triangle and bear. Buyers can also request exclusive artwork subject to the applicable custom MOQ and sampling review.

Q4: What is the MOQ for private-label orders?

HUIJIA PET's current program starts from 100 pcs for eligible standard styles and simple branded packaging, 200 pcs for logo application, 300 pcs for exclusive printed artwork and 500 pcs for a fully custom structure or hardware. Final MOQ is confirmed by specification.

Q5: What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the selected pattern or artwork, buckle type, quantity by size, logo method, packaging requirements, target market and requested delivery date. Current production timing and pack-out are confirmed with the quotation.
