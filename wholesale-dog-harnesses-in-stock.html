BUYER FAQ

Ready-Stock Dog Harness Questions

Direct answers about inventory, MOQ units, mixed SKUs, dispatch estimates, private label and quality control.

Are all dog harnesses on this page currently in stock?

The green Ready Stock label is used only for styles managed under the ready-stock program. Exact color, size and quantity availability, plus the estimated dispatch date, are confirmed by SKU at quotation because inventory changes.

What is the ready-stock wholesale MOQ?

The listed ready-stock styles start from 30 pcs or 30 sets per style. The applicable sales unit and MOQ are shown on each product card and product page, then confirmed in the quotation.

What is the difference between pcs and sets in the MOQ?

Pcs refers to individual harnesses. Sets refers to bundled products such as a harness with a matching leash. Use the unit shown on the selected product page when preparing the quantity breakdown.

Can we mix colors and sizes in a ready-stock dog harness order?

Yes. Ready-stock wholesale can mix available colors and sizes, with at least 3 pcs or sets per selected SKU combination. Current inventory is confirmed before order.

How do we receive current inventory and an estimated dispatch date?

Send the selected style, quantity by color and size, target market and delivery destination. HUIJIA PET will confirm current inventory by SKU, the applicable sales unit and the estimated dispatch date in the quotation.

Can ready-stock harnesses use private-label packaging or logos?

Private-label packaging and logo options are available under separate order tiers. Simple branded packaging starts from 100 pcs per style and logo application from 200 pcs per style; the selected method, inventory and lead time are confirmed during quotation.

How is bulk dog harness quality controlled?

Quality control can include incoming material checks, in-line workmanship inspection, measurement and fit checks, stitching and hardware review, pull-strength testing against buyer requirements, final inspection and packing verification.
