BUYER FAQ

Custom Adjustable Nylon Dog Harness Manufacturer Questions

Direct answers for pet brands and wholesale buyers planning a custom program.

What webbing can be used for an adjustable nylon harness?

Nylon or polyester webbing can be selected in different widths, colors, hand feels and finishes according to the product specification and target price.

Can the webbing include our logo or pattern?

Yes. Options can include woven labels, printed webbing, rubber patches and coordinated colors, subject to artwork and MOQ review.

What is the MOQ for an adjustable nylon dog harness?

Selected existing styles start from 100 pcs per style. Custom colors or branding typically start from 300 pcs per style per color, and fully custom development typically starts from 500 pcs per design.

What information is needed for sampling?

Provide a drawing, reference sample or photos, requested webbing width, size range, colors, logo artwork, hardware direction, estimated quantity and packaging request.

What is the typical production lead time?

Bulk production normally takes 25–35 days after sample approval. Special webbing, custom hardware or complex packaging may require 35–45 days.
