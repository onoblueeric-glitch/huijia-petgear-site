BUYER FAQ

Custom Printed Dog Collar Manufacturer Questions

Direct answers for pet brands and wholesale buyers planning a custom program.

Can you make a dog collar from our artwork?

Yes. Buyers can provide artwork, a repeat pattern, brand guidelines or reference images. Print direction, colors and components are confirmed before sampling.

Can a printed dog collar have padding?

Yes. Printed webbing can be developed with a padded or soft-lined construction where suitable for the selected style.

Can printed collars be matched with leashes and harnesses?

Yes. The same artwork, color palette, logo details, webbing and packaging can be coordinated across a full collection.
