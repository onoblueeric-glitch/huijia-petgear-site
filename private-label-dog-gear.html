ONE MANUFACTURING PROGRAM

From One Product to a Complete Walking Collection

Private label should mean more than applying a logo to an existing item. A differentiated dog walking range connects product structure, material, color, hardware, branding, packaging and target price. We help organize these decisions into a specification that can move from sample to production.

Brands can begin with one lead product, such as a custom dog harness, and expand into matching leashes and collars. Developing the range together improves visual consistency and gives retailers or distributors a more complete collection to purchase.

Product Development
