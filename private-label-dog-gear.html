BEFORE YOU START

Private-label questions, answered.

Need a specification reviewed? Send your product references and the details you already have.

Discuss your project ↗
Can I add my logo to an existing style?

Yes. Start with a product reference and tell us your preferred logo position and packaging. We will review the suitable branding method, artwork size and order quantity for that style before quoting.

What is the MOQ for a private-label order?

MOQ depends on the product, customization and quantity split. Many listed walking-gear styles start from 100 pieces or 100 sets; the starting quantity on a product card does not automatically cover custom colors, logos or packaging. Confirm the requirement per style, color and size in your quotation.

Can I mix sizes, colors or different products?

Send your proposed quantity split for each product, color and size. We will review the combination against material, branding and packaging requirements. A harness, collar and leash may have separate order requirements even when they form one collection.

What files do I need for a quotation?

Share product links or reference photos, the size range, estimated quantities, target market and launch date. Include your logo or print artwork if available. A vector logo, color references and a packaging brief help us define the specification.

How are samples, fees and lead times confirmed?

Sample cost and timing are quoted after the design, materials, logo method and packaging are reviewed. Confirm any tooling or setup costs and whether a sample fee can be credited to a bulk order. Bulk timing is agreed after sample approval and the final order specification.

Can you develop matching harnesses, collars and leashes?

Yes. We can coordinate color, print, branding and hardware across a walking collection. Specify which items belong in each set, their sizes and whether they need individual or combined retail packaging.

What retail packaging can I request?

Options include hangtags, belly bands, inserts, pouches, boxes and barcode labels. Share your artwork, required label information, pack dimensions and carton marks so that packaging can be included in the sample and quotation.

What is the difference between private label, OEM and ODM?

Private label can start with an existing style and your branding. An OEM project follows your product specification or reference sample, while an ODM project includes product-development support. The materials, design changes and approval steps are agreed for each project.
