PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Build a Harness for Your Market

A successful custom dog harness needs more than a logo. Fit, pull direction, webbing width, padding, adjustment range, clip position and size grading all affect the final product. We review these details against your intended use, whether the range is designed for everyday walking, puppies, active dogs, working dogs or fashion retail.

Your harness can also be developed as the lead product in a matching collection. Colors, prints, hardware finishes and labels can be coordinated with a leash, collar and retail packaging so buyers see one complete brand story.

No-Pull Harnesses

Front and back clip structures with adjustable straps, handles and padded contact areas.

Step-In & Vest Harnesses

Easy-wear constructions for small dogs, puppies, fashion ranges and daily walking.

Mesh & Neoprene Harnesses

Breathable or cushioned materials selected for comfort, season and target price.

Tactical & Outdoor Harnesses

Reinforced webbing, handles, metal hardware and reflective details for active ranges.

Custom Branding

Matching Accessories
