Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HUIJIA PET handles personal information when you visit huijiapetgear.com, submit a business inquiry, or contact our sales team by email or WhatsApp.

We may collect information that you choose to provide, including your name, business email address, company, target market, product requirements, estimated quantity, files or reference materials, and the contents of your message. If you contact us through WhatsApp or another communication service, that service may also process your account and communication data under its own privacy policy.

Our website and hosting providers may automatically receive limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, timestamps, and security or diagnostic logs.

How We Use Information

  • Communicate about product development, orders and customer support.

How Information Is Shared

We may share information with personnel and service providers that support hosting, communications, inquiry handling, product development, manufacturing, logistics, professional advice or legal compliance. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or complete a corporate transaction.

International Processing

HUIJIA PET serves customers internationally. Your information may be processed in countries other than the country where you are located. We take reasonable steps to use information only for the business purposes described in this policy.

Retention and Security

We retain inquiry and business correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or active business relationship, maintain records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system or transmission is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information that you provided, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements. You may also ask us to stop nonessential business communications.
